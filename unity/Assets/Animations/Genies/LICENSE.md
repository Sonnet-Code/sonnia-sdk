# Genies animations

Every clip in this folder was made by Genies for their avatar skeleton and is
redistributable. They are copied here so that "Sonnia > Create Motion Set" can set
their loop and root settings and strip the events of their original controllers.

- `Wave` and `Peace`: Genies Avatar Starter Pack
  (https://github.com/geniesinc/GeniesStarterPack), under the notice kept in
  `Assets/Avatar/Genies/LICENSE.md`.
- `actions-presentItem_intro` and `actions-0224-lookAtObject_Trimmed`: GeniesIRL
  (https://github.com/geniesinc/GeniesIRL, `Assets/Project/Animation`).
- `QuickAction_salute`, `QuickAction_twoThumbsUp` and `QuickAction_vibe`: GeniesCamera
  (https://github.com/geniesinc/GeniesCamera, `Assets/Project/Animation`).

GeniesIRL and GeniesCamera are © Genies, Inc. and licensed under the Apache License 2.0;
the full text is in `LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt`.

## Derived clip

`Assets/Animations/Sonnia/SonniaIdle.anim` is generated by "Sonnia > Create Motion Set"
from the first frame of `actions-presentItem_intro` (GeniesIRL, Apache-2.0), held still
with a slow weight shift added on top. It is a modified version of that clip and is
distributed under the same Apache License 2.0.

## Not included

The Genies Avatar SDK ships its own common animations (`DefaultIdle`, `Walk`...) under
the Genies SDK license (https://legal.genies.com/sdk-eula.html). They are not part of
this repository. When the SDK is installed, "Sonnia > Create Motion Set" copies them to
`Assets/Animations/GeniesSdk`, which must not be committed.
